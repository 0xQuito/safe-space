# Safe Space

An idea for an online safeguarding project I'm working on, after hearing the horror stories of children being targeted online.

The idea is to build simple tools that kids can easily access when something online doesn't feel right. It's a concept, but one I hope is helpful.

## Try it

Check it out on your own device:

**About** (right side of this repo) → click the link

Or go straight to: **https://0xquito.github.io/safe-space/**

## What it does

- Helps a child write down what happened, step by step, in simple language
- Lets them save the report as a PDF or email it to a parent, teacher or someone they trust
- Shows helpline numbers for their country
- Keeps everything private: nothing is sent anywhere unless they choose to share it
